AI Researcher

Daniel McDuff

I build machine learning systems that sense, model, and synthesize human behavior, with an emphasis on health, physiology, affective computing, and responsible deployment.

My research spans camera-based physiological measurement, multimodal affect recognition, wearable foundation models, and health agents that transform longitudinal sensor data into useful personal and clinical insight.
